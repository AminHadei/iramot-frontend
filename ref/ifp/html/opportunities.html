Shape the Future with Us

Join a team that’s building a more abundant and prosperous American future.

At the Institute for Progress, we conduct research and advocate for policies that accelerate scientific, technological, and industrial progress. We’re looking for people who embody the values of policy entrepreneurship: intellectually curious, operationally excellent, and motivated by impact.

Pitch a Job

Don’t see a job that fits, but have an idea for how you could move IFP’s mission forward?

Email hiring@ifp.org with your CV and a short note describing the role you’d create.

Benefits

Great healthcare

We cover 100% of employee health insurance premiums and 50% for dependents, with optional dental and vision plans.

Unconditional 403(b) contribution

We contribute 5% of your salary to your retirement plan automatically — no employee match required.

Unlimited PTO

We have an unlimited PTO policy, and encourage our staff to take off at least two weeks per year (in addition to the team holidays outlined below!).

Generous team holidays

We follow federal OPM holidays, close the week between Christmas and New Year’s, and often add extra days throughout the year.

Parental leave

All full-time staff receive three months of fully paid parental leave, regardless of gender.

Keep up to date with IFP
Each month, we’ll send you a monthly roundup of IFP research, news, and updates.
